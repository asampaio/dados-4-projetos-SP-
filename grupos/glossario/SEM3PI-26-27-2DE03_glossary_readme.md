# 📖 Project Glossary

Quick reference guide for the domain-specific concepts of the **ShopRobot** project. It is kept consistent with the domain model (conceptual level) and focuses on business meaning. Each entity has an **Identity**: the attribute that identifies it in the business domain. Value objects ([Address](#address), [Coordinate](#coordinate), [Polygon](#polygon)) have no identity of their own.

## 💼 Domain & Business Terms

### A - D

* <a id="address"></a>**Address**
  * **Definition:** Postal location of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location): street, postal code and city.
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Location](#shopping-location)
  * **Note:** Value object. It has no identity: it is defined by its values and always belongs to one [Shopping Location](#shopping-location). The requirements only mention an address, so its structure is a proposal.
* <a id="area"></a>**Area**
  * **Definition:** Any delimited space inside a [Level](#level) of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) (a shop, a corridor, a toilet, a parking zone, an elevator, etc.). It is described by a name, a [category](#area-category) and a [polygon](#polygon) (formed by [coordinates](#coordinate) taken from the [GeoJSON](#geojson) layout) that marks its position in the level.
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Level](#level), [Area Category](#area-category), [Polygon](#polygon), [Store](#store), [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot)
  * **Rule:** A [Store](#store) is always located in an Area of category *shop*.
  * **Rule:** A [Robot Station](#robot-station) may be located in an Area. When it is, the Area must be on the same [Level](#level) as the station. An Area holds at most one Store or one Robot Station.
  * **Note:** Names can be empty or repeated (e.g., corridors), so the name cannot identify an Area. The identifier is the `id` property of the [GeoJSON](#geojson) feature and is never generated by the database.
* <a id="area-category"></a>**Area Category**
  * **Definition:** Classification of an [Area](#area) according to its use. It comes from the [GeoJSON](#geojson) layout, and the list is open (not complete), so it is stored as free text instead of a fixed list. Examples: `amenity` (toilets, food_court, parking, post_office, bank, pharmacy, bureau_de_change, restaurant), `indoor` (corridor, room), `shop` (clothes, sports, bag, fashion_accessories), `highway` (elevator).
  * **Related concepts:** [Area](#area)

* <a id="backoffice-application"></a>**Back-office application**
  * **Definition:** Computer program used by the [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator) (maybe the Merchant too, to be confirmed with the client) to import the stores and levels of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) from a [GeoJSON](#geojson) file and to create the [Stores](#store) available at that [Shopping Location](#shopping-location).
  * **Related concepts:** [GeoJSON](#geojson), [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator), [Shopping Location](#shopping-location), [Store](#store)
  * **Rule:** It has a text-based UI.
  * **Rule:** It accesses the relational database.
  * **Rule:** It is capable of importing JSON data.
* <a id="battery-level"></a>**Battery Level**
  * **Definition:** Current charge of a [Robot](#robot), expressed as a percentage (0 to 100). It limits the trips the robot can make: if one robot's charge is not enough for the whole [Route](#route), another robot is looked for, or the [Order](#order) is split into [Sub-orders](#sub-order). Robots with low battery must return to a [Robot Station](#robot-station) to charge.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Robot State](#robot-state), [Route](#route), [Sub-order](#sub-order)
  * **Note:** The client stated that the minimum battery required for an [Order](#order) is not in the acceptance criteria: it can be an input parameter or a global property (an indicative value is 20–25%). It is not stored in the model. The unit is not defined in Sprint 1 (a percentage in the data files); the client said it may change in the future. Route planning is not part of Sprint 1.

* <a id="control-terminal"></a>**Control Terminal**
  * **Definition:** Interface installed at each [Robot Station](#robot-station) used for its management tasks: consulting capacity and parked robots (and their [state](#robot-state)), changing a robot's state (e.g., setting it in maintenance) and viewing the schedule of robots entering or leaving the station.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Maintenance Operator](#maintenance-operator)
  * **Note:** Not modeled as a class. It is a feature of the [Robot Station](#robot-station).
* <a id="coordinate"></a>**Coordinate**
  * **Definition:** A point in the layout of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location), given by its longitude and latitude (in this order, as in [GeoJSON](#geojson)). It is a vertex of a [Polygon](#polygon).
  * **Related concepts:** [Polygon](#polygon), [GeoJSON](#geojson)
  * **Note:** Value object. It has no identity: two coordinates with the same longitude and latitude are the same.
* <a id="customer"></a>**Customer**
  * **Definition:** Person who uses the Shopping [Front-end application](#shopping-frontend-app) to browse the [Stores](#store) and [Products](#product) joined to ShopRobot, fill a [Shopping Cart](#shopping-cart) and place an [Order](#order). The customer can be anywhere (restaurant, cinema, even at home) while the robots do the shopping.
  * **Identity:** `id` (the customer ID used to filter orders).
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Cart](#shopping-cart), [Order](#order), [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot)

* <a id="delivery-spot"></a>**Delivery Spot**
  * **Definition:** Place inside the [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) where the [Customer](#customer) receives the order from the [Robot](#robot). It is chosen by the Customer when confirming the [Order](#order) and is represented by an [Area](#area).
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Area](#area), [Stop](#stop)
* <a id="delivery-time"></a>**Delivery Time**
  * **Definition:** Time requested by the [Customer](#customer) for the order to be delivered (`requestedDeliveryTime` in the model). It acts as the deadline used to prioritize pending orders.
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order)

### E - M

* <a id="elevator"></a>**Elevator**
  * **Definition:** [Area](#area) of category `highway: elevator` that connects the [Levels](#level) of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location). [Robots](#robot) must use elevators to change floors.
  * **Related concepts:** [Area](#area), [Level](#level), [Robot](#robot), [Shopping Location](#shopping-location)
  * **Note:** Not a separate class. Level changes in a [Route](#route) are derived from the sequence of [Stops](#stop).

* <a id="geojson"></a>**GeoJSON**
  * **Definition:** File format used to describe the layout of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location). It is the source from which the [Levels](#level), [Areas](#area) and [Stores](#store) are imported by the back-office application. It is a data source, not a business concept of the model.
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Location](#shopping-location), [Level](#level), [Area](#area), [Polygon](#polygon), [Coordinate](#coordinate)

* <a id="level"></a>**Level**
  * **Definition:** A floor of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) (e.g., levels 0 to 3 of NorteShopping). A shopping location is a volume made of several levels, served by elevators. Each level contains [Areas](#area) and usually one [Robot Station](#robot-station) (except customer parking levels).
  * **Identity:** `number`, unique within its [Shopping Location](#shopping-location).
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Location](#shopping-location), [Area](#area), [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Elevator](#elevator)
  * **Rule:** A Level has at most one [Robot Station](#robot-station).
  * **Note:** The number is a decimal. It can be negative (e.g., a basement) or fractional (e.g., 0.5), because some levels of the [GeoJSON](#geojson) file are decimal numbers.

* <a id="maintenance-operator"></a>**Maintenance Operator**
  * **Definition:** Actor responsible for servicing [Robots](#robot) and [Robot Stations](#robot-station). Uses the [Control Terminal](#control-terminal) to check the parked robots and to set a robot in maintenance.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Robot State](#robot-state), [Control Terminal](#control-terminal)
* <a id="merchant"></a>**Merchant**
  * **Definition:** Actor that represents the owner of one or more [Stores](#store) that joined [ShopRobot](#shop-robot). Defines the [Products](#product) available to order through the [Shopping Front-end Application](#shopping-frontend-app), which can differ from what is sold directly in the physical store.
  * **Identity:** `email`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Store](#store), [Product](#product), [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator)
  * **Rule:** A Merchant is assigned to a [Store](#store) by the [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator) after the [Store](#store) is created. A Store without a Merchant has no [Products](#product).

### N - R

* <a id="order"></a>**Order**
  * **Definition:** Purchase confirmed by a [Customer](#customer) from the [Shopping Cart](#shopping-cart), with a requested [Delivery Time](#delivery-time) and [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot). It can include [products](#product) from several [Stores](#store), and is fulfilled by one [Robot](#robot) or, if needed, by several Robots through [Sub-orders](#sub-order).
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Cart](#shopping-cart), [Customer](#customer), [Order Line](#order-line), [Sub-order](#sub-order), [Order Status](#order-status), [Robot](#robot), [Route](#route)
  * **Rule:** An Order is fulfilled either directly by one [Robot](#robot) or through its [Sub-orders](#sub-order), never both. The same applies to its [Route](#route).
  * **Rule:** An Order has at least one [Order Line](#order-line).
  * **Rule:** An Order is final once it is confirmed and paid: it cannot be changed after payment (client decision).
  * **Note:** Total weight, total volume and the need for refrigeration are not stored. They are derived from the [Order Lines](#order-line) and their [Products](#product) (operations of the Order in the model).
* <a id="order-line"></a>**Order Line**
  * **Definition:** Item of an [Order](#order): one [Product](#product) and the ordered quantity. The unit price is recorded at the time of purchase (it is the [Product Price](#product-price) in force). A line is always carried whole: it is never divided between [Sub-orders](#sub-order).
  * **Identity:** `lineNumber`, unique within its [Order](#order).
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Product](#product), [Product Price](#product-price), [Sub-order](#sub-order)
* <a id="order-partitioning"></a>**Order Partitioning**
  * **Definition:** Division of an [Order](#order) into [Sub-orders](#sub-order) when no single [Robot](#robot) can fulfil it. It respects the weight, volume and refrigeration of the items, and groups whole [Order Lines](#order-line).
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Sub-order](#sub-order), [Refrigerated Unit](#refrigerated-unit)
* <a id="order-status"></a>**Order Status**
  * **Definition:** Stage of an [Order](#order) or [Sub-order](#sub-order) in its life cycle: `PENDING` (waiting to be assigned), `ASSIGNED` (a [Robot](#robot) was chosen), `IN_DELIVERY` (robots are collecting or delivering), `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Sub-order](#sub-order)
  * **Note:** Values are a proposal, to be confirmed with the client.

* <a id="polygon"></a>**Polygon**
  * **Definition:** Closed shape that marks the position and boundary of an [Area](#area) in the layout of its [Level](#level). It is formed by at least three [Coordinates](#coordinate) (its vertices), as read from the [GeoJSON](#geojson) file.
  * **Related concepts:** [Area](#area), [Coordinate](#coordinate), [GeoJSON](#geojson)
  * **Note:** Value object. It has no identity and always belongs to one [Area](#area).
* <a id="product"></a>**Product**
  * **Definition:** Item that a [Store](#store) makes available to order through [ShopRobot](#shop-robot), with a [price](#product-price) (which has a history), weight, volume and whether it needs refrigeration. Weight, volume and refrigeration are what determine which [Robots](#robot) can carry it.
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Store](#store), [Product Price](#product-price), [Order Line](#order-line), [Refrigerated Unit](#refrigerated-unit)
  * **Rule:** Changing the price of a Product never changes the price paid in previous [Orders](#order).
  * **Note:** The current price is not stored in the Product: it is the latest [Product Price](#product-price) (operation `currentPrice()` of the Product in the model).
* <a id="product-price"></a>**Product Price**
  * **Definition:** Price of a [Product](#product) from a given date and time on. A Product has a history of prices, and its current price is the one with the latest date.
  * **Identity:** `validFrom`, unique within its [Product](#product).
  * **Related concepts:** [Product](#product), [Order Line](#order-line)
  * **Rule:** A price change is a new Product Price. The previous ones are kept and are never changed or deleted, so the [Order Lines](#order-line) already placed keep the unit price paid (rule of the BDDAD teacher: a retail system keeps a price history).
  * **Rule:** A [Product](#product) has at least one Product Price.

* <a id="refrigerated-unit"></a>**Refrigerated Unit**
  * **Definition:** [Robot](#robot) capable of transporting [Products](#product) that require refrigeration. A Robot that is not refrigerated cannot carry them, so orders mixing both kinds have to be split.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Product](#product), [Order Partitioning](#order-partitioning)
  * **Rule:** A refrigerated [Robot](#robot) carries only [Products](#product) that require refrigeration, and a non-refrigerated Robot carries only Products that do not. There are no mixed robots (client decision).
* <a id="retailer"></a>**Retailer**
  * **Definition:** Company that owns one or more [Shopping Locations](#shopping-location). Its administrator defines which [Stores](#store) are available in the system.
  * **Identity:** `name`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Shopping Location](#shopping-location), [Store](#store), [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator)
* <a id="retailer-administrator"></a>**Retailer Administrator**
  * **Definition:** Actor who represents a [Retailer](#retailer) and decides which [Stores](#store) of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) are made available in [ShopRobot](#shop-robot). Distinct from the [Merchant](#merchant), who only manages the products of the stores already joined to the system.
  * **Related concepts:** [Retailer](#retailer), [Store](#store), [Merchant](#merchant)
  * **Note:** Not modeled as a class. The back-office application is used by this actor to import the layout from [GeoJSON](#geojson).
* <a id="robot"></a>**Robot**
  * **Definition:** Electric autonomous vehicle that collects the products of an [Order](#order) in the [Stores](#store) and delivers them to the [Customer](#customer). It has a unique identification, a [state](#robot-state), a [battery level](#battery-level), and a maximum volume and weight it can transport. It may be a [refrigerated unit](#refrigerated-unit). It parks in a [Robot Station](#robot-station) when not in use or when in need of charging its battery.
  * **Identity:** `id`, the unique identification of the robot.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot State](#robot-state), [Battery Level](#battery-level), [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Order](#order), [Sub-order](#sub-order), [Route](#route)
  * **Rule:** A Robot that is delivering is not parked in any [Robot Station](#robot-station).
  * **Rule:** When a Robot's battery charge decreases to a specific low percentage (yet to be determined alongside the client), the Robot heads to the nearest [Robot Station](#robot-station) to charge.
* <a id="robot-state"></a>**Robot State**
  * **Definition:** Current operational situation of a [Robot](#robot): `READY` (idle in a station and available for orders), `IN_USE` (satisfying an order), `CHARGING` (charging in a station), `MAINTENANCE` (unavailable for orders).
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Robot Station](#robot-station)
  * **Rule:** Only `READY` robots can be assigned to orders.
  * **Note:** The requirements describe the states in prose (in use, in maintenance, idle, charging) and the assignment story (USEI04) uses `BUSY`. The team adopted `READY` (idle) and `IN_USE` (the `BUSY` of the story). The client confirmed that a state for a robot that is working on an order ("busy") is allowed, so these names are kept.
* <a id="robot-station"></a>**Robot Station**
  * **Definition:** Place where [Robots](#robot) park when they are not in active use. They can charge their batteries and also be maintained there. Each station has a limited capacity (number of robots it can hold) and a [Control Terminal](#control-terminal). Usually there is one per [Level](#level), except in customer parking levels. It may be located in an [Area](#area) of its Level.
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Level](#level), [Area](#area), [Control Terminal](#control-terminal)
  * **Note:** Occupied and free spots are not stored: they are derived from the parked robots and the capacity (operations of the station in the model).
  * **Note:** The position of a station is optional because the requirements do not say where stations are. It becomes mandatory only if route planning needs it.
* <a id="route"></a>**Route**
  * **Definition:** Ordered sequence of [Stops](#stop) a [Robot](#robot) follows to collect the products of an [Order](#order) or [Sub-order](#sub-order) across [Stores](#store) and deliver them at the [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot). Planning a route means deciding the best sequence of stops. It has an estimated distance and an estimated duration.
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Stop](#stop), [Robot](#robot), [Order](#order), [Sub-order](#sub-order), [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot)
  * **Rule:** A Route belongs either to an [Order](#order) (when one robot does it all) or to a [Sub-order](#sub-order), never both.
  * **Note:** No Sprint 1 user story plans routes. The route calculation belongs to the Planning application, which uses the [Area](#area) polygons from the [GeoJSON](#geojson) layout.
  * **Note:** Route planning is done by the system with input from an operator: the operator sets the request, the system proposes the route and the operator accepts or rejects it. The navigation of the [Robot](#robot) itself (avoiding obstacles and following the path) is autonomous and out of scope.

### S - Z

* <a id="shopping-cart"></a>**Shopping Cart**
  * **Definition:** Temporary selection of [Products](#product) a [Customer](#customer) is building while browsing [Stores](#store), before confirming it. Becomes an [Order](#order) (with its [Order Lines](#order-line)) once the Customer confirms the purchase with a [Delivery Time](#delivery-time) and [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot).
  * **Related concepts:** [Customer](#customer), [Product](#product), [Order](#order)
  * **Note:** Not modeled as a class. It only exists in the Shopping application until the purchase is confirmed.
* <a id="shopping-frontend-app"></a>**Shopping Front-end Application**
  * **Definition:** The computer program the [Customers](#customer) will use to place [Orders](#order). The [Customer](#customer) can use it to list all the available [Stores](#store) and select all the [Products](#product) they want to purchase, and the [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) and time they want it delivered.
  * **Related concepts:** [Customer](#customer), [Order](#order), [Store](#store), [Product](#product), [Shopping Location](#shopping-location)
  * **Rule:** It has a text-based UI.
  * **Rule:** The app accesses the relational database.
* <a id="shopping-location"></a>**Shopping Location**
  * **Definition:** Specific place where customers shop and the solution operates (outlet, shopping center, large hypermarket), e.g., NorteShopping. It has an [address](#address), is made of several [Levels](#level), and a [Retailer](#retailer) can own several.
  * **Identity:** `name`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Retailer](#retailer), [Level](#level), [Address](#address)
* <a id="shop-robot"></a>**ShopRobot**
  * **Definition:** The name of the project here being developed and also of the entire program solution that will result from it, and whose business scope is here documented.
* <a id="stop"></a>**Stop**
  * **Definition:** A single point in a [Robot's](#robot) [Route](#route): either a [Store](#store) where products are collected, or the [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot) where the order is handed to the [Customer](#customer).
  * **Related concepts:** [Route](#route), [Store](#store), [Delivery Spot](#delivery-spot)
  * **Note:** Not modeled as a class in the current domain model. See [Route](#route). Out of scope for Sprint 1 (no Sprint 1 user story plans routes), documented now to avoid terminology drift.
* <a id="store"></a>**Store**
  * **Definition:** Physical shop of a [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) that joined [ShopRobot](#shop-robot). It is located in one [Area](#area) and sells [Products](#product) through the [Shopping Front-end Application](#shopping-frontend-app). Not every shop in the shopping location is a Store of the system.
  * **Identity:** `name`, unique within its [Shopping Location](#shopping-location).
  * **Related concepts:** [Area](#area), [Merchant](#merchant), [Product](#product), [Retailer](#retailer), [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator)
  * **Rule:** A Store created by the import of the [GeoJSON](#geojson) file has no [Merchant](#merchant) until the [Retailer Administrator](#retailer-administrator) assigns one.
* <a id="sub-order"></a>**Sub-order**
  * **Definition:** Part of an [Order](#order) created only when no single [Robot](#robot) can fulfil the order, due to volume, weight, refrigeration or battery. Each sub-order groups some of the order's [lines](#order-line) and is carried by its own Robot. A simple order has no sub-orders.
  * **Identity:** `id`.
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Order Line](#order-line), [Robot](#robot), [Order Partitioning](#order-partitioning), [Order Status](#order-status), [Route](#route)
  * **Rule:** Every [Order Line](#order-line) of a split order belongs to exactly one Sub-order, and every Sub-order has at least one line.

## 👥 Other Actors (not modeled as classes)


* <a id="public"></a>**Public**
  * **Definition:** People who share the [Shopping Location](#shopping-location) with the [Robots](#robot) while they navigate autonomously. They are affected by the robots' behavior, which is why each robot shows its status and next destination on a screen and with a light.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Shopping Location](#shopping-location)

* <a id="system-operator"></a>**System Operator**
  * **Definition:** Actor who operates the solution day to day, following the state of the [Robots](#robot), the [Robot Stations](#robot-station) and the [Orders](#order) being fulfilled.
  * **Related concepts:** [Robot](#robot), [Robot Station](#robot-station), [Order](#order)

* <a id="system-planner"></a>**System Planner**
  * **Definition:** Actor who plans the deliveries before they happen: decides how the products of an [Order](#order) are loaded onto the [Robots](#robot) and the sequence of [Stops](#stop) of each [Route](#route).
  * **Related concepts:** [Order](#order), [Order Partitioning](#order-partitioning), [Route](#route), [Stop](#stop)
