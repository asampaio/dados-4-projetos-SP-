# 📖 Project Glossary

Quick reference guide for domain-specific concepts used across the project.

## 💼 Domain & Business Terms

| Term                                 | Term(PT)                     | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|:-------------------------------------|------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Area                                 | Área                         | A zone of a level represented by coordinates and classified by type. It includes stores, corridors, toilets, parking, elevators,etc.                                                                                                                                                                   |                |
| Area Type                            | Tipo de área                 | Classification of an area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Autonomous Navigation                | Navigação autónoma           | Movement of the robot inside the shopping location without human intervention, in the presence of the public                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Battery Level                        | Nível de bateria             | Current charge of the robot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Charging                             | Carregamento                 | Recharging of a robot's battery at a station.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Control Terminal                     | Terminal de controlo         | Terminal at each station used for management tasks: checking capacity and parked robots, changing robot states and viewing the movement schedule                                                                                                                                                       |
| Corridor                             | Corredor                     | Circulation area through which robots move between stores and delivery points                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| CSV                                  | CSV                          | Plain text file used to store table data (like rows and columns) ready to be imported                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Customer                             | Cliente                      | User of the Shopping application who browses stores and products, and fulfills purchases. They may be inside the mall itself or at home                                                                                                                                                                |
| Delivery Location                    | Local de Entrega             | Specific point inside the shopping location where the robot hands the order over to the customer                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Delivery Time/Deadline               | Hora/Prazo de entrega        | Moment by which the customer wants to recieve the order. It is the main criteria for the queue of pending orders                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Elevator                             | Elevador                     | Means of connecting levels. Robots must use it to change floors                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| GeoJSON                              | GeoJSON                      | File format used to describe the layout of the shopping location (levels and areas)                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Infrastructure planner               | Planejador de infraestrutura | Actor responsible for preparing and configuring the operational basis of the ShopRobot system within a shopping location.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Level                                | Nível/Piso                   | A floor of a shopping location. It contains areas and is connected to the other levels by elevators.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Logistics Operator                   | Operador de Logistica        | Actor responsible for the day-to-day fulfillment of customer orders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Maintenance                          | Manutenção                   | Servicing of robots and stations, performed by the Maintenance Operator                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Maintenance Operator                 | Operador de Manutenção       | Actor who services robots and stations and uses the station's control terminal                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Max Volume/Max Weight                | Volume/Peso máximo           | Load limits a robot can carry                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Merchant                             | Comerciante                  | Actor linked to a store who manages the products available for purchase through ShopRobot                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Order                                | Encomenda                    | Purchased confirmed by the customer, with a delivery time and location. Its products are collected from the stores and delivered by one or more robots. Has a delivery deadline and belongs to a customer                                                                                              |
| Order Item                           | Item de encomenda            | A line of an order: a product(and quantity) to be collected.It has properties relevant to transport (weight,volume,refrigeration requirement)                                                                                                                                                          |
| Pending order                        | Encomenda pendente           | An order that has been confirmed but not yet processed, kept in a queue sorted by the earliest delivery deadline                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Product                              | Produto                      | Item that the customer can buy through the application. The catalogue is specific to this selling channel and may differ from what is sold directly in the store. It is defined by the Merchant                                                                                                        |
| Public                               | Público                      | People present in the shopping location who share the space with the robots                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Refrigerated product                 | Produto refrigerado          | Product that requires refrigerated transport and can therefore only be carried by robots with a refrigerated unit                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Refrigerated Unit                    | Unidade refrigerada          | Robot capable of carrying products that require refrigeration                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Retailer Administrator               | Store Manager                | Actor responsible for setting up and maintaining a shopping application in the ShopRobot system, using the back-office application.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Robot                                | Robô                         | Autonomous eletric vehicle that collects products from stores and delivers them to customers. It has a unique identification,maximum capacity(volume and weight), a refrigeration indicator, a state and a battery level. It has an information screen(status and next destination) and a status light |
| Robot Station (also parking station) | Estação de robôs             | Location within the shopping location where robots park , and charge their batteries, when they are not in active use                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Robot Status                         | Estado do robô               | Predefined set of various states that describe the robots current situation                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Shopping Location                    | Shopping                     | A commercial venue where customers shop and robots patrol, such as an outlet, a shopping center or a hypermarket,                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Shopping application                 | Aplicação de compras         | The application used by customers to browse the stores and products available in a shopping location, fill a shopping cart                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Store (also Shop)                    | Loja                         | Commercial area located on a specific level of a shopping location, where products are sold.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
